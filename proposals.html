How it works

  1. 1 · Propose

    Open a proposal on GitHub. Say what, why, who stewards it, what it costs, and what would end it. Short and concrete beats long and complete.

  2. 2 · Discuss

    Talk it through in the issue and at Thursday Office Hours. Leave a 👍 to show support. Edit the proposal as it gets better.

  3. 3 · Decide

    Members decide at the monthly close, on the first Thursday of the month. A majority of members carries it. The decision is recorded in the issue, and the status label changes to active or declined.

  4. 4 · Run

    An active proposal with a cost becomes an active expense on the books. Its steward posts a one-line update at each monthly close.

  5. 5 · Review

    Every quarter, members keep it, change it, or discontinue it.
