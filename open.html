Working in the Open

What we do in public, how to check it, and how to take part.

What's open

Money
Every payment in and out of the treasury is on the BSV public ledger.
See the books →
Code
This website and our tools are open source. Read it, fork it, or send a fix.
GitHub →
Infrastructure
bitblocks is a free, MIT-licensed BSV explorer and API. Anyone can use it.
bitblocks.app →
Conversation
Office Hours are open to everyone, every week. The chain is for what happened. Discord is for what's happening.
Discord →

Why

Check, don't trust
Trust should come from what you can check, not from who says it. A public ledger lets anyone check.
Show the work
Bugs, drafts, and failed experiments stay visible. Critics become collaborators when they can see the process.
Grow the commons
Shared code doesn't run out. Others improve it and give the improvements back.

Start small

  1. 1 Look up one transaction in our books.
  2. 2 Come to one Office Hours and see what we're working on.
  3. 3 Make one thing of your own public: a tool, a lesson learned, or a roadmap.
